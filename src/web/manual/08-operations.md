# Operations

The parts of Meridian that are not the ledger.

## CRM

**Leads** are unqualified interest. Qualifying one turns it into a customer and
an opportunity.

**Opportunities** carry a value, a stage, a probability and an expected close.
The **pipeline** is a board — drag a card to move a stage. The **forecast**
weights each opportunity by its probability and lays the next few months out.

**Activities** are calls, meetings, emails and tasks, against any record.

**Cases** are support requests with a thread, a priority and an owner.

**Campaigns and partners** attribute where business came from, and
**commissions** calculate what is owed on it.

## Projects

A project has a customer, a manager, a budget, tasks and a billing method.

- **Time entries** book hours against a project and a task, at a billing rate
  that can vary by employee, by role or by project.
- **Expenses** attach to a project and can be marked billable.
- **Resource allocation** shows who is committed to what, and where the gaps
  and the double-bookings are.
- **Billing** turns unbilled time and expenses into an invoice. A project
  needs either a service item or an income account, so the invoice knows what
  it is selling.

Percentage-of-completion, fixed fee and time-and-materials are all supported.

## Service

For businesses that send somebody out.

- **Service assets** are the things you maintain, at customer sites.
- **Service contracts** cover them, with renewal dates the dispatch screen
  watches.
- **Service orders** are the jobs: scheduled to a technician, carrying parts
  and labour lines.
- **Dispatch** is the day board — who is where, what is unassigned, who is
  free.

Parts issued on a service order come out of the technician's van stock if they
have one, and post cost of sale properly rather than vanishing.

## People

- **Employees** with departments, managers, salaries and an org chart.
- **Time off** requests with approval and balances.
- **Attendance and shifts** for hourly workforces.
- **Payroll runs** calculating gross, employee tax withheld, employer tax and
  net, posting the lot to the ledger.
- **Performance reviews** on a cycle, with competencies, ratings and goals.

## Commerce

**Sales channels** represent a web store or a marketplace. **Listings** publish
items to a channel with their prices. **Carts** arrive from the channel and
convert into sales orders — abandoned carts stay visible, because that is the
number the screen exists for.
